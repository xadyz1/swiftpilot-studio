# Hungry Pockets source notes

Reviewed https://hungrypockets.eu/ and https://hungrypockets.eu/manifesto?lang=en on 6 November 2026. Hungry Pockets is a streetwear brand, with the tagline “Always Starving Since 2014.” Its manifesto describes a chest-pocket origin, ambition and drive, and an evolution toward utility and urban armor. The homepage links the official accounts https://instagram.com/hungrypockets and https://tiktok.com/@hungrypockets.

Products shown include Five Panel Cap, Dad hat, Women’s crop top, Unisex windbreaker, Brand Oversized faded t-shirt, and Cuffed Beanie. Marketing templates avoid time-sensitive prices and unverified scarcity or discounts. Planned dates in the app are editable content suggestions, not commitments or published posts.

Original lifestyle and logo sources: https://hungrypockets.eu/assets/img/man.webp, https://hungrypockets.eu/assets/img/women.webp, https://hungrypockets.eu/assets/img/logowhite.png, https://hungrypockets.eu/assets/img/appicon.png. Product image URLs were extracted directly from the official homepage, which serves its Printful product mockups. Copies are in client/public/brand so downloads can use same-origin images.

The app is a simple browser-local planner. Calendar changes and template edits persist in localStorage, not a shared cloud database. Scheduling means planning; publishing is manual. Downloadable graphics are branded still-image compositions; video concepts include shooting notes rather than finished video files.
